UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE WESTERN DISTRICT OF TENNESSEE
AFFIDAVIT IN SUPPORT OF APPLICATION FOR SEARCH AND SEIZURE WARRANTS
CASE: OPERATION MEMPHIS LAW FIRM (MOROLTO SYNDICATE RACKETEERING)

I, Special Agent F. Denton Voyles, Federal Bureau of Investigation, being duly sworn, depose and state:
1. For the past twenty-two months, the FBI Organized Crime Strike Force has conducted an undercover investigation into Bendini, Lambert & Locke, a tax boutique law firm located in Memphis, Tennessee.
2. Evidence establishes that the firm is a captive corporate conduit owned and controlled by Joey Morolto [ID:P401], head of the Morolto Crime Family in Chicago.
3. Managing Partner Oliver Lambert [ID:P402] and Enforcer Partner Nathan Locke [ID:P403] systematically operated Account 010 / ACC-BENDINI-MEMPHIS and Account 010 / ACC-LOCKE-SPECIAL to receive millions in cash casino skims, funneling them offshore into Account 010 / ACC-CAYMAN-TRUST operated by Avery Tolar [ID:P407].
4. Intercepted telecom records (CDR) demonstrate that wire executions between 01:00 AM and 03:00 AM were directly preceded by encrypted phone calls between Accused P401, P402, and P403.
5. Cooperating witness Mitchell Y. McDeere [ID:P404] has provided physical custody of internal double-billing records and Cayman corporate ledgers establishing violations of 18 U.S.C. 1961, 18 U.S.C. 1956, and mail fraud.
