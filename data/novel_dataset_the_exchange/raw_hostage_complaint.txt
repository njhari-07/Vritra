INTERNATIONAL CRIMINAL COURT & SPECIAL COUNTER-TERROR TRIBUNAL
INVESTIGATION REPORT: ADHEEM BARAKAT DESERT KIDNAPPING SYNDICATE
CASE REF: ICC-2024-EXCHANGE-LY-099

1. SUMMARY OF AMBUSH & SYNDICATE COMMAND:
Following the ambush at the Great Gaddafi Bridge in Tripoli, associate attorney Giovanna Sandroni was abducted by a Libyan militant faction commanded by Adheem Barakat [P601].
Adheem Barakat operates as the syndicate Kingpin, dictating $100,000,000 sovereign ransom demands.
Extortion demands and proof-of-life recordings were routed by Barakat's operative Noura [P602] to Abby McDeere in Manhattan.
Lannak Construction CEO Omar Celik [P603] and Geneva controller Adam Celik [P608] colluded with the kidnappers to extract a fraudulent $400M settlement from the Libyan treasury, receiving an agreed $20M kickback.
Driver Samir [P609] deliberately steered the legal convoy into the desert ambush perimeter.
Mitch McDeere [P604], Giovanna Sandroni [P605], Abby McDeere [P606], and Luca Sandroni [P607] are designated witnesses and victims.
